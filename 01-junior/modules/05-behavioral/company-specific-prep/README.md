# 🏢 Company-Specific Interview Preparation

## 🎯 FAANG + Elite Tech Company Deep Dive

Master the unique interview styles, cultural values, and technical focuses of top-tier technology companies.

## 🔥 Google - Technical Excellence + Googleyness

### **Interview Format**
- **Technical Rounds:** 2-3 coding interviews + 1 system design (L4+)
- **Behavioral:** Googleyness & Leadership assessment
- **Focus:** Algorithm mastery, code quality, scalability thinking

### **Googleyness Principles**
- **Mission-driven:** User impact and technical innovation
- **Collaborative:** Cross-functional teamwork and communication
- **Comfortable with ambiguity:** Systematic approach to unclear problems
- **Learning mindset:** Continuous growth and adaptation

### **Go-Specific Preparation**
- Study Go usage at Google (infrastructure, Kubernetes, etc.)
- Understand Google's Go style guide and best practices
- Practice implementing Google-scale algorithms in Go
- Review Go concurrency patterns for distributed systems

### **Sample Behavioral Questions**
- "Tell me about a time you had to work with ambiguous requirements"
- "Describe a technical decision you made that had broad impact"
- "How do you approach learning new technologies?"

## 📅 Company-Specific Preparation Timeline

**4 weeks before interviews:**
- Research company-specific culture and values
- Adapt behavioral stories to company principles
- Study company's technical blog and engineering posts

**2 weeks before interviews:**
- Practice company-specific behavioral questions
- Review company's Go usage and architecture patterns
- Mock interviews focused on company style

**1 week before interviews:**
- Final review of company values and recent news
- Practice elevator pitch tailored to company mission
- Prepare thoughtful questions about company's technical challenges
